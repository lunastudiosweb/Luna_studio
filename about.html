LUNA STUDIOS

About Luna.

A studio built to bring independent projects together without taking away what makes them unique.

01

WHAT WE ARE

One studio. Different ideas.

Luna Studios is a parent organization for independent websites, projects, and ideas. Each branch exists for its own purpose and is operated by its own owner.

Luna Studios provides a shared identity and a place for these projects to exist within the same network.

02

INDEPENDENT BRANCHES

Built by different people.

A Luna Studios branch is not simply another page of the same project. Each branch can have its own owner, direction, design, and community.

The connection between them is Luna Studios. The projects themselves remain independent.

03

THE NETWORK

Connected, not identical.

Luna Studios brings independent projects into one network while allowing each of them to remain recognizable as its own.
